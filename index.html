        Neighbor viewFor growers and households next to a sprayed block
        Why this exists: growers we met at a Tucson farmers market told us organic farms move far out to avoid overspray, and that they would want to know what is happening near their land and water. This is a notice built from the tower readings and the applicator's own start button. Wind measured at one tower does not establish where droplets or vapor travel, so this is not a drift map.
